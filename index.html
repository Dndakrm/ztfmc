DUAL-LAYER GEOPOLITICS

Manage territory, claim chunks, and collect city upkeep with Towny, while operating tactical war bands, trade guilds, or bandit syndicates with BetterTeams.

PLAYER-DRIVEN TRADE

No infinite admin handouts. Raw logs, ores, and crops are seeded through player labor, with decentralized player chest shops driving the true server economy.

AUTOMATED BOUNTIES

Wanderers are protected by automated karma laws, while notorious criminals face server-wide bounties. Hunt the hunted or reign as public enemy #1.

SURVIVAL INTEGRITY

Strict survival administration. Staff play by the exact same resource constraints and vulnerability rules as all citizens—no creative cheats or unfair advantages.
