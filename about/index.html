CHRONICLES OF ZEFTY

OUR STORY

Built by two friends. Powered by community. Survived through time.

ORIGINS

THE BEGINNING

ZEFTY MC started back in 2024 by two longtime friends known as ZEDD and FROSTY[cite: 13].

The name ZEFTY was born directly from combining their online handles: ZEdd + FrosTY[cite: 13].

Originally, the server was never meant to become a public community[cite: 13]. It was simply a small SMP created for pure survival fun — just two friends wanting an unscripted world to explore, construct, and enjoy together in vanilla Minecraft[cite: 13].

As time passed, more travelers joined the realm[cite: 13]. Alliances forged, empires rose, and what began as a humble private realm transformed into a thriving civilization[cite: 13].

2024: THE ROOTS

ZEFTY MC was first founded as an intimate private survival server dedicated to authentic, grind-heavy survival between close friends[cite: 13].

COMMUNITY ERA

Word traveled across the community, transforming the quiet multiplayer world into a public hub of diverse players, factions, and shared trade[cite: 13].

S7: SOVEREIGN

Returning from a year-long hiatus, the founders reconstructed ZEFTY into Season 7: Project Sovereign — a true, dual-layer medieval civilization[cite: 13].

OUR CREED

MORE THAN A SERVER

ZEFTY MC was never engineered to operate like a bloated corporate network filled with pay-to-win microtransactions[cite: 13].

It is a world built by players, for players — governed by community merit, unscripted wars, and honest survival grit[cite: 13].

Years later, the original two creators remain at the helm, maintaining server integrity and continuing the journey alongside every citizen who steps foot in the realm[cite: 13].
