CITIZEN SURVIVAL GUIDE

HOW TO SURVIVE & RULE

No complex plot maintenance debt or confusing wand tools. Stake your grief-free territory using classic fence boundaries, establish sovereign realms, and dominate the world.

1. THE FIRST TEN MINUTES

SURVIVAL COMMENCEMENT

You awaken at the Royal Citadel as a [Wanderer]. Before establishing a mighty kingdom, prepare your basic survival inventory:

I

Wilderness Warp

Run /rtp to teleport straight into untouched survival biomes far away from spawn.

II

Ration Kit

Equip yourself with starting stone armaments and sustenance using /kit starter.

III

Earn Balance

Level your AuraSkills attributes or sell gathered mining spoils at /shop to fund your land claims.

2. LAND CLAIMING (PAGAR & SIGN)

CLASSIC REDPROTECT TERRITORY SETUP

Grief protection on Project Sovereign uses the standard Indonesian server claiming method: enclose your property with any wooden fence and place an [rp] sign. No wand selections or complex coordinates needed.

How to Write the Sign:

1. Enclose your base completely with a continuous perimeter of wooden fences (any wood type).
2. Place a regular wooden sign directly against any fence post.
3. On the 1st line, write [rp].
4. On the 2nd line, write your desired ClaimName.
5. Leave lines 3 and 4 blank, then hit Done.

The sign will automatically colorize into dark blue text, lock your chests, and establish grief prevention up to the sky and bedrock.

Claim Management Commands

Command Target Description
/rp addmember <player> Ally / Friend Grant building, chest, and door access inside your claim.
/rp removemember <player> Ex-Member Revoke build and storage permissions immediately.
/rp delete Current Claim Permanently unclaim the territory you are currently standing in.
/rp info Current Claim Display claim owner, coordinates, block size, and active flags.
Automatic Sovereign Protection

Inside your fence perimeter, creeper craters, fire tick, chest looting, and unauthorized block breaking are completely disabled. Your build is 100% safe.

3. SOVEREIGN NATIONS

FACTION SYSTEM & CITIZEN COMMANDS

Unite multiple settlements under one national banner. Joining or creating a nation updates your chat and TAB badge from [Wanderer] to your nation's royal crest.

Command Permitted Role Action
/n create <name> Wanderer Establish a new nation ($100 balance). You become the Sovereign Leader.
/n invite <player> Leader Invite an online player to swear allegiance to your kingdom.
/n join <name> Citizen Accept a pending invite and automatically trust into national RedProtect claims.
/n sethome Leader Designate your capital's warp coordinates.
/n home All Citizens Teleport back to the national capital after a 3-second channel.
/n info [name] Anyone Inspect population count, founder, and status of any nation.
/n leave Citizen Renounce your citizenship and return to [Wanderer] status.
/n disband Leader Permanently dissolve your realm and wipe all citizen affiliations.

4. BEDROCK & STREAM-READY CROSSPLAY

ACCESSIBLE TOUCH COMMANDS

Designed specifically for live streams and mobile crossplay players who want fast, responsive survival without getting bogged down by complicated server commands:

Shortcuts

Use single-letter aliases like /n instead of typing /nation on mobile touch keyboards.

One-Click Invites

Whenever a leader invites you to a realm, tap the green [ACCEPT] prompt directly in your chat box.

Proximity Comms

Hear allies and enemies directly through Simple Voice Chat during open-world exploration and kingdom wars.
