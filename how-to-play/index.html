HOW TO PLAY

Master sovereign mechanics to defend your settlement, secure territories, and harness arcane power across the realm.

1. FOUNDING & EXPANDING YOUR TOWN

All territory claiming is managed through Towny. You must officially found a settlement before staking claims in the wild.

/t create <town_name> Founds your initial town settlement and establishes your town homeblock. Must be run before claiming any surrounding wilderness.
/t deposit <amount> Deposits your personal balance into your town's central treasury. Land expansion requires municipal funds to complete.
/t claim Claims the chunk you are standing on for your town. Each land expansion costs 25 balance deducted directly from the town bank.

Automatic Explosion Rollback

If a creeper or explosion damages your claimed land, don't panic! Towny features automated terrain regeneration: the cratered ground and your destroyed chest contents will automatically rebuild and restore back to their original state.

2. CUSTOM ENCHANTMENTS

You do not need awkward GUI menus or complex altars to get endgame power. Custom enchantments are integrated directly into standard vanilla mechanics—simply construct a regular enchanting table setup surrounded by bookshelves to roll sovereign enchantments.

Standard 15-bookshelf arrangement unlocks tier-3 custom rolls.
Unique sovereign combat, utility, and harvesting enchants revealed on roll.
