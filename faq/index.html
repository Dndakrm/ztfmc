CITIZEN ARCHIVES

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Find immediate answers regarding territory boundaries, sovereign realms, Bedrock connectivity, and server mechanics.

How do I claim and protect my land?

We use the classic fence-and-sign method via RedProtect. Enclose your perimeter completely with any wooden fence, attach a wooden sign to any post, write [rp] on line 1, and your desired claim name on line 2. Once placed, the text turns blue and your land is fully protected from sky to bedrock.

Will my claim expire or get deleted from upkeep taxes?

No. Unlike Towny, there is no daily midnight upkeep tax or bank maintenance debt. You never have to worry about your town dissolving because you went on vacation or missed a daily payment. Your claim remains secure as long as you are an active member of our community.

What are Sovereign Nations and how do they work?

Nations are organized coalitions of players. Found one using /n create <name> ($100 in-game balance) to replace your [Wanderer] status with your kingdom's crest in TAB and chat. Leaders can recruit citizens using /n invite <player>, set national rally points with /n sethome, and travel back anytime via /n home.

Can Bedrock (Mobile / PE / Console) players play smoothly?

Yes! We run native Geyser and Floodgate integration on Paper 1.21.x. Bedrock players connect using play.zeftymc.com with the default Bedrock port 19132. All commands are optimized with short single-letter aliases (e.g., /n) and clickable chat buttons so you don't have to type long strings on touchscreen keyboards.

Can other players grief my chests or blow up my base?

Not inside your claimed land. Inside your fence perimeter, RedProtect automatically locks all storage containers (chests, barrels, hoppers) and negates creeper explosions, TNT damage, and unauthorized block tampering. Outside claimed territory, the wilderness is open survival.

How do I earn money to establish a nation or expand?

You can earn in-game currency through multiple survival activities:

  • Leveling up your skills through AuraSkills (mining, foraging, combat).
  • Selling resources in the server market using /shop.
  • Trading goods directly with other players in community towns.

How do I give a friend build access to my house?

Stand inside your claim and type /rp addmember <player>. To revoke access, use /rp removemember <player>.
