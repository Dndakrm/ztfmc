What versions can join?

The world runs natively on 1.21 with backwards compatibility supporting clients from 1.18 up to the latest release. For the smoothest gameplay, latest block models, and optimal visual fidelity, client versions 1.20 and above are strongly recommended.

Is ZEFTY MC free / cracked friendly?

Yes. Both official premium Mojang/Microsoft accounts and offline/cracked players can join. Official accounts authenticate automatically, while non-premium players secure their identity through our password registration system.

Is griefing allowed?

Unclaimed wilderness has no protection and is subject to open-world survival risks. However, land claimed under a Towny settlement is protected. In the event of accidental creeper detonations or mob attacks inside your borders, our rollback system will automatically reconstruct the destroyed blocks and restore the contents of damaged chests.
